Before you hire

Six checks that take ten minutes

Every installer we pass on should clear these. It's still worth doing them yourself.

REGISTER

Look them up yourself

Search the installer on the FENSA, MCS, NICEIC/NAPIT or Gas Safe Register website rather than trusting a logo on a van.

ID CARD

Ask for the card on the day

Gas engineers carry a Gas Safe ID card listing exactly which appliances they're qualified for. Check the back.

QUOTE

Get it itemised, in writing

Equipment brand and model, labour, scaffolding, waste removal and VAT should each be a separate line.

DEPOSIT

Keep the deposit small

25% or less is normal. Paying by credit card for jobs over £100 adds Section 75 protection.

GUARANTEE

Insist on an IBG

An insurance-backed guarantee covers the workmanship warranty if the firm stops trading.

CERTIFICATE

Get the sign-off paperwork

Notifiable work needs a Building Regulations certificate. Without it, selling or remortgaging can stall.

Grants & rules

Money off and legal must-haves

Scheme values change. Check the current rules on GOV.UK or with the Energy Saving Trust before you sign.

Solar · heat pumps · insulation0% VAT

Energy-saving materials installed in homes are zero-rated until 31 March 2027. The rate is due to rise to 5% from 1 April 2027, so book surveys early if timing matters.

Heat pumps · England & Wales£7,500

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme takes £7,500 off an air or ground source heat pump. Your MCS-certified installer applies for the voucher on your behalf.

Solar exportsSEG

The Smart Export Guarantee pays you for electricity you send to the grid. You need an MCS certificate and a smart meter, and rates vary by supplier.

Landlords · England5 yrs

Private landlords need an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) at least every five years, and an annual gas safety record for every gas appliance.

Questions

Common questions

Is the service really free?

Yes. Homeowners never pay us. Installers pay a small fee per introduction, which never appears on your quote.

My double glazing has gone misty. Do I need new windows?

Usually not. Condensation between the panes means the sealed unit has failed. Replacing just the glass unit costs far less than a new window and keeps the existing frame.

Do solar panels need planning permission?

Most roof-mounted systems on houses in England are permitted development. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flat roofs can need permission, and your installer should check before quoting.

Can any electrician replace my fuse box?

A consumer unit replacement is notifiable under Part P in England and Wales. Use an electrician registered with a competent person scheme such as NICEIC or NAPIT so they can self-certify the work.

Who can work on my air conditioning or heat pump?

Anyone handling refrigerant must hold an F-Gas certificate. For heat pumps that qualify for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, the installer must also be MCS-certified.

How fast can someone come out for a heating breakdown?

Choose "Urgent" on the form. In most areas a Gas Safe engineer can attend within 24–48 hours.
